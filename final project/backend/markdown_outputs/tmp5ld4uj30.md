Main Project – Spectra ASD: Autism Screening Platform
Developed a web-based intelligent screening system designed to identify early signs of Autism Spectrum Disorder 
(ASD).
The platform integrates machine learning (ADTree model) with behavioral questionnaire data provided by 
caregivers to predict potential ASD traits.
It generates analytical reports that assist psychologists and healthcare professionals in early detection and 
intervention.
An extended version includes real-time video analysis to observe non-verbal behaviors such as eye contact 
duration, facial attention patterns, and social responsiveness, helping improve assessment accuracy.
The system aims to provide a cost-effective and accessible early screening solution for families and healthcare 
centers.
