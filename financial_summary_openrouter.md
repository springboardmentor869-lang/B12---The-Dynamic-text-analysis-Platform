* **Section Overview**: This document is the World Intellectual Property Organization's (WIPO) annual financial report and financial statements for the year ending December 31, 2020.

* **Key Details & Insights**:
    *   The report includes an introduction, financial statement discussion and analysis, annual statement on internal control, independent auditor's report, and the financial statements themselves.
    *   The financial statements have been prepared in accordance with International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS).
    *   WIPO's mission is to lead the development of a balanced and effective global intellectual property ecosystem.
    *   WIPO generates most of its revenue from fees paid by users of its intellectual property services, primarily through the Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT), Madrid, and Hague systems.
    *   The COVID-19 pandemic significantly impacted WIPO's operations, leading to the closure of its Geneva headquarters and a shift to remote working.
    *   WIPO's Risk Management Policy sets out the Organization's approach to managing risks and internal controls.
    *   Daren Tang was appointed as the new Director General on May 8, 2020, and began his term on October 1, 2020, succeeding Mr. Francis Gurry.

* **Financial Performance 2020**:
    *   WIPO's surplus for the year was 135.9 million Swiss francs, compared to 97.7 million Swiss francs in 2019.
    *   Total revenue was 468.3 million Swiss francs, an increase of 2.5% from 457.0 million Swiss francs in 2019.
    *   Total expenses were 365.8 million Swiss francs, a decrease of 8.9% from 401.4 million Swiss francs in 2019.
    *   Investment gains were 33.4 million Swiss francs, compared to 42.1 million Swiss francs in 2019.
    *   PCT system fees accounted for 76.6% of total revenue, rising by 6.1% compared to 2019, totaling 358.6 million Swiss francs.
    *   Madrid system fees represented 16.3% of total revenue, falling by 0.8% compared to 2019, totaling 76.2 million Swiss francs.
    *   Hague system fees increased from 5.3 million Swiss francs in 2019 to 6.7 million Swiss francs in 2020.
    *   Voluntary contributions decreased by 46.8% compared to the prior year, totaling 5.8 million Swiss francs.
    *   Personnel expenditure was 233.7 million Swiss francs, representing 63.9% of total expenses, a decrease of 3.6% compared to 2019.
    *   Contractual services totaled 88.6 million Swiss francs, a decrease of 7.8% compared to 2019.
    *   Travel, training, and grants expenses decreased by 89.7% due to the COVID-19 pandemic, totaling 1.8 million Swiss francs.

* **Financial Position 2020**:
    *   Net assets were 387.1 million Swiss francs, with total assets of 1,390.9 million Swiss francs and total liabilities of 1,003.8 million Swiss francs.
    *   Cash, cash equivalents, and investment balances totaled 932.0 million Swiss francs, representing 67.0% of total assets.
    *   Fixed assets (land, buildings, intangible assets, and equipment) had a total net book value of 382.5 million Swiss francs.
    *   Employee benefit liabilities were 495.3 million Swiss francs, mainly comprised of the ASHI liability of 452.8 million Swiss francs.
    *   Payables and advance receipts totaled 435.3 million Swiss francs, including deferred revenue for international applications of 289.1 million Swiss francs.

* **Cash Flow 2020**:
    *   The cash, cash equivalents, and investments balance increased by 177.9 million Swiss francs.
    *   Monthly cash inflows from PCT system fees averaged 30.7 million Swiss francs.
    *   The medium-term investment portfolio (core cash investments) had a fair value of 471.7 million Swiss francs.
    *   The long-term investment portfolio (strategic cash investments) had a fair value of 200.6 million Swiss francs.

* **Annual Statement on Internal Control**:
    *   The Director General is accountable for maintaining a system of internal financial control.
    *   The system of internal control aims to manage risk to a tolerable level.
    *   WIPO published monthly crisis management dashboards on its website.
    *   The Risk Management Group (RMG) reviews and monitors WIPO's financial situation.

* **Independent Auditor's Report**:
    *   The auditor's opinion states that the financial statements present fairly the financial position of WIPO as of December 31, 2020, and its financial performance and cash flows for the year then ended in accordance with IPSAS.
    *   The auditor's opinion also states that the revenue and expenses have been applied to the purposes intended by the General Assembly and the financial transactions conform to the Organization's Financial Regulations.
