* **Section Overview**: This document is the World Intellectual Property Organization's (WIPO) annual financial report and financial statements for the year ending December 31, 2020.

* **Key Details & Insights**:
    *   The report adheres to Regulation 6.7 of WIPO's Financial Regulations and Rules (FRR) and is prepared in accordance with International Public Sector Accounting Standards (IPSAS).
    *   The report includes an overview of WIPO's operations, financial objectives, risk management, and the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic.
    *   WIPO's mission is to lead the development of a balanced and effective global intellectual property ecosystem.
    *   The main policy and decision-making bodies of WIPO are the General Assembly, the Conference, and the Coordination Committee.
    *   Daren Tang was appointed as the new WIPO Director General on May 8, 2020, and began his six-year term on October 1, 2020, succeeding Francis Gurry.
    *   WIPO generates most of its revenue from fees paid by users of its intellectual property services, with the PCT system being the largest contributor.
    *   The Program and Budget for the 2020/21 biennium was approved in October 2019.
    *   WIPO's Risk Management Policy emphasizes organization-wide responsibility.
    *   The COVID-19 pandemic led to the closure of WIPO's Geneva headquarters on March 17, 2020, with a phased return starting in June 2020 and a resumption of remote working in November 2020.
    *   COVID-19 related expenditure totaled approximately 3.6 million Swiss francs, mainly for IT equipment and services.
    *   Revenue from the PCT system was 6.1% higher than in 2019, totaling 358.6 million Swiss francs.
    *   PCT application filings grew by 4.0% in 2020, reaching an estimated 275,900 applications.
    *   Revenue from the Madrid system fell by 0.8% compared to 2019, totaling 76.2 million Swiss francs.
    *   International trademark applications decreased by 0.6% to an estimated 63,800.
    *   Revenue from the Hague system increased from 5.3 million Swiss francs in 2019 to 6.7 million Swiss francs in 2020.
    *   The accumulated annual leave liability increased by 5.8 million Swiss francs due to travel limitations.
    *   WIPO achieved investment gains of 33.4 million Swiss francs in 2020.
    *   The surplus for the year was 135.9 million Swiss francs, with total revenue of 468.3 million Swiss francs and total expenses of 365.8 million Swiss francs.
    *   Total revenue increased by 2.5% compared to 2019.
    *   Total expenses decreased by 8.9% compared to 2019.
    *   Net assets increased by 22.9 million Swiss francs during 2020.
    *   Employee benefit liabilities totaled 495.3 million Swiss francs, mainly comprised of the ASHI liability of 452.8 million Swiss francs.
    *   The ASHI liability increased by 139.1 million Swiss francs compared to 2019.
    *   As at December 31, 2020, payables and advance receipts totaled 435.3 million Swiss francs, including deferred revenue of 289.1 million Swiss francs.
    *   The cash, cash equivalents, and investments balance increased by 177.9 million Swiss francs in 2020.
    *   The Annual Statement on Internal Control concludes that there were no material weaknesses affecting the reliability of the financial statements.
    *   The Independent Auditor's Report provides an unqualified opinion on the financial statements and their regularity.
    *   The report includes detailed financial statements: Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Financial Performance, Statement of Changes in Net Assets, Statement of Cash Flow, and Statement of Comparison of Budget and Actual Amounts.
